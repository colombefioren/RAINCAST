# 03:00 Madagascar time), built with build_kernel_queue.py --gpu: two T4 GPUs, one run each, in priority order.
# The molab sessions of 23 October showed that every from-scratch recipe tried lands at the same validation
# level (last-k average with tuned thresholds: 0.710 to 0.712 for U-Net++ resnet34 at 12 to 16 epochs, for the
# resnet50 encoder and for batch 32 with learning rate 2e-3; 0.709 for the plain U-Net), and that schedules
# past 16 epochs overfit. So these sessions add ensemble members, not new recipes: validation and full-data
# pairs of the strongest variants (U-Net++ resnet34 at 14 epochs, its best at 0.7117; resnet50 and the plain
# U-Net for diversity). Validation seeds are 8xx, each full-data twin is the seed + 100 (9xx).
# Expected on T4 (about 365 s per U-Net++ resnet34 epoch with validation): 1.2 to 2 hours per U-Net++ run,
# under an hour per U-Net run; the launcher skips the runs that would not end within the budget.
