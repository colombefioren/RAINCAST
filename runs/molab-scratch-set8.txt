# Eighth molab session (25 October): cross-season fold models. Plain 14-epoch U-Net++ runs that hold out one of
# the three other training seasons (--val_season 2019, 2020 or 2021) instead of December 2022 to March 2023, six
# seeds per season, seasons interleaved so that a cut session still covers all three. Their validation
# probabilities are out of fold for those seasons, which gives (1) calibration and decoder settings fitted on four
# seasons instead of one, and (2) about 8,000 more samples of honest ensemble probabilities to train a second-stage
# model on (the context calibration gained +0.001 on held-out days of the single validation season). The test side
# stays the full-data twins. Two runs at a time, like the seventh session.
